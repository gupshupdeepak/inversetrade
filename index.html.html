FAQ

How does the inverse strategy actually work? +

You log your intended trade (“BUY BTC at $67,000”). The extension instantly logs the mirrored order — “SHORT BTC at $67,000” — in your paper portfolio, with the same entry price and timer. If your instinct is wrong 80% of the time, the mirror is right 80% of the time.

Is this real money? Do you connect to my broker? +

No and no. InverseTrade is paper trading only. It never connects to a broker, never asks for API keys, and has no code path that can place a real order.

Does it work on any trading platform? +

The timer and journal work anywhere you can open Firefox. Automatic chart-price detection currently targets Zerodha Kite, TradingView and ChartIQ-style legends. If auto-detection fails, you can click the price on the chart or type it manually.

What if I’m actually a good trader? +

Then your journal will show the inverse losing — which is a useful, humbling signal. The extension doesn’t force you to trade against yourself; it makes the comparison brutally visible.

How do I install it on Firefox? +

Head to the Setup Guide. It’s a two-minute install from the Firefox Add-ons store — no config files, no sideloading.

Can I export my flipped trade history? +

Yes — every logged flip can be exported as CSV with entry/exit prices, direction, duration, P&L in points, P&L percent and status. Bring it to your journal, spreadsheet, or therapist.

Install on Firefox — Free
Setup · ~2 minutes

Install InverseTrade & flip your first trade

No config files. No API keys. No real orders. Here’s the whole thing in five steps.

Install the Firefox add-on

Open Firefox and go to the official Firefox Add-ons page. Search for InverseTrade, or click the “Add to Firefox” button on this site.

Firefox will show a permissions prompt. InverseTrade only requests storage (to save your paper trades locally) and access to trading pages you open. It cannot execute real orders.

Pin the panel to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Firefox’s toolbar, find InverseTrade, and select Pin to Toolbar.

Open any charting page — Zerodha Kite, TradingView, your broker’s web terminal — and the floating InverseTrade panel will appear in the top-right corner of the page.

Log the trade you want to make

In the panel, type the instrument (or click “Detect chart price” to auto-fill it), choose BUY or SELL, set a duration (5–60 minutes), and press Start trade.

Example: you see BTC breaking out and want to BUY at $67,420 for 25 minutes.

The extension flips it automatically

The instant you log the trade, InverseTrade mirrors it — your intended BUY becomes a paper SHORT (and vice versa) at the same entry price and with the same timer.

When the timer ends, the extension auto-detects the live chart price and closes the mirrored trade, logging P&L in points and percent.

If auto-detection fails on a canvas-only chart, the panel prompts you to click the price on the chart or type it manually. Nothing is ever placed in your real account.

Read your inverse journal

The Recent trades list shows each flipped trade with direction, entry, exit and P&L. Hit Export CSV to bring your flipped history into a spreadsheet, trading diary, or journal app.

Do this for a week. If your inverse P&L is consistently green while your gut calls were red — congratulations, you just found an edge. If it isn’t, at least you learned your instinct is better than you thought.

Troubleshooting

  • Panel doesn’t appear? Reload the trading tab. Some pages load the extension only after a hard refresh (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).
  • Chart price not detected? Click “Detect chart price” again, highlight a price on the page, or type the price manually in the field.
  • Timer ended while tab was closed? InverseTrade will not auto-detect a stale price. It asks you to enter the exit price manually so your journal stays honest.
  • Nothing works? Head to the contact page — include your Firefox version and the site you were trading on.
